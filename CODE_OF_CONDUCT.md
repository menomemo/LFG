# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

This matters more than usual here. LFG is a tool for people looking for work, often in a
country that is not the one they grew up in. Visa status, language ability, and the gaps in
someone's CV are the substance of this project — and they are also the things people are
most often treated badly about. Discussions here will touch all of them.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about ideas and generous about people. Criticise a design, not the person who
  proposed it.
- Assume the other person knows something you do not. A question about a market you have
  not searched in is a question, not a correction.
- Accept feedback on your own work the same way you would like yours received.
- When you are wrong, say so plainly and move on.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Comments demeaning someone's nationality, immigration status, accent, language ability,
  or career gap
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission — including
  anything about an employer, recruiter, or interview that identifies a real person who did
  not consent to being discussed here
- Sustained disruption of discussions
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages —
and whenever someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer. Contact details will be added
here when the repository is made public; until then, use GitHub's private reporting on this
repository.

Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer will respond with whatever is
proportionate: a private word, a public correction, removing a comment, or a temporary or
permanent ban. The maintainer is a single person and will say so if they are too close to a
situation to handle it fairly.
